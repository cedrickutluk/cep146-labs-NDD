# Scrambled Eggs

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- A small amount of cooking oil
- A little salt

## Cooking Steps

1. Break the eggs into a bowl.
2. Add a little salt.
3. Mix the eggs.
4. Heat some oil in a pan.
5. Pour the eggs into the pan.
6. Stir the eggs until they are fully cooked.

**Important tip: Use medium heat so the eggs do not burn.**
# Seneca Academic Integrity Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to maintain a high standard of **academic integrity** at Seneca. It provides rules for research, teaching, learning, and working.

## Scope

This policy applies to current Seneca students and employees.

## Key Definitions

### Academic Integrity

> Academic integrity means being honest, fair, respectful, responsible, trustworthy, and courageous in academic work.

### Cheating

**Cheating** means using dishonest methods to receive credit or improve a grade. Examples include copying work, using unauthorized help, impersonating another student, or using AI without permission.

### Contract Cheating

**Contract cheating** happens when a student submits work completed by another person or service as their own work.

### Plagiarism

**Plagiarism** means using another person's words, ideas, images, or other work without giving proper credit to the source.

### Self-Plagiarism

**Self-plagiarism** means reusing your own work from an earlier assignment without permission.

### Unauthorized Aid

An **unauthorized aid** is help that the instructor has not approved. Examples include another student, notes, a smartphone, the internet, or an AI tool.

### Undeclared AI Use

**Undeclared AI use** means using artificial intelligence for an assignment without explaining that AI was used and how it was used.

## Policy

The four main sections are:

- **General**
- **Academic Integrity Program**
- **Responsibilities**
- **Sanctions**

### 1. General

Seneca supports honesty, trust, fairness, respect, responsibility, and courage. The college wants students to learn in a positive, fair, and inclusive environment.

### 2. Academic Integrity Program

Academic integrity helps make sure that a Seneca credential represents a student's own knowledge and skills. Seneca supports academic integrity through policies, training, and student programs.

### 3. Responsibilities

Seneca must give students information and resources about academic integrity. Students should tell their professor if a situation may affect their academic performance. Students may also report a suspected academic integrity violation.

### 4. Sanctions

Sanctions depend on how serious the violation is. Seneca may review the student's record and circumstances before making a final decision.

Possible sanctions include:

- A verbal or written warning
- Completing an academic integrity assignment
- Redoing an assignment or assessment
- Receiving a lower grade
- Failing the course
- Suspension from Seneca
- Permanent expulsion for a serious later offence
